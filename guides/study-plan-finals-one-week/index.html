How to make a study plan for finals when you have one week

Updated November 16, 2026

In short: SkyTasks is a free planner for students where you add an exam and it suggests study sessions in the free gaps of your week, so a one-week plan takes about two minutes to set up. Without an app, the same plan works on paper: count back from the exam date, give each day one topic and one short session, and keep the last two days for past papers only.

The plan, step by step

  1. Write the exam date first. Everything else is counted back from it. If you have several finals, write all of them before you plan a single session.
  2. List the topics, not the chapters. A topic is something you could be asked about in one question. Ten to fifteen topics is normal for one course.
  3. Give each day one or two topics. One session of 45 to 60 minutes a day beats one long night. Put the hardest topic on the first day, while there is still time to ask someone.
  4. Keep the last two days for past papers. Doing old exams under time shows you the weak spots. Fix only those, and stop studying new material.
  5. Plan around your real week. Classes, work and travel are already there. The study sessions go into the gaps, not on top of them.

How it looks in SkyTasks

You type the exam as one sentence, for example "biology final on Thursday". SkyTasks shows a countdown to the day and suggests study times in the free gaps of your week, around your class timetable and the calendars already on your phone. You can accept the suggestions or pick your own hours. Each session is a task with a reminder, and every session you finish adds a floor to the school building of that exam in your city. A week later you can see how ready you are, instead of guessing.

Common questions

Is one week enough to study for a final?

For most courses, yes, if you study every day and start with the topics you understand least. One week is not enough to learn a whole course from zero, so the plan above assumes you attended at least part of the classes.

How many hours a day should I study for finals?

One or two focused sessions of 45 to 60 minutes, with a break between them, is what most students can keep up for a week. More hours on day one usually means fewer hours on day five.

Which app makes a study plan for finals?

SkyTasks does it from one sentence: add the exam, and it suggests study sessions in your free hours with a countdown to the day. It is free on iPhone and Android, with your phone calendars and class timetable in the same view.

What if I miss a planned session?

Move it, do not stack it. In SkyTasks a task that passed its time moves to today on its own, so you decide in the morning where it fits, and the plan stays one session a day.
